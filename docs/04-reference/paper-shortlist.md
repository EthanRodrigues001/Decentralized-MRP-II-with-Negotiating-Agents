Only papers 1 and 11 explicitly use MRP II in their titles or central scope. The other papers support individual MRP II layers such as distributed production planning, supplier coordination, capacity allocation, negotiation, finite scheduling, manufacturing control, and disruption handling. They should not be presented as complete MRP II implementations.

## IEEE Xplore

### 1. The Methods of Improving the Manufacturing Resource Planning (MRP II) in ERP

- Authors: Wenchao Jiang and Jingti Han
- Year and venue: 2009, International Conference on Computer Engineering and Technology
- DOI: [10.1109/ICCET.2009.18](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCET.2009.18)
- Relevance: **Direct MRP II reference.** Useful for establishing that MRP II covers wider manufacturing resources and ERP integration, although it does not focus on decentralized agents.

### 2. A Multi-Agent System Framework for Manufacturing Planning and Control

- Authors: T. N. Wong, C. W. Leung, and H. P. Tang
- Year and venue: 2008, 7th World Congress on Intelligent Control and Automation
- DOI: [10.1109/WCICA.2008.4592959](https://doi.org/10.1109/WCICA.2008.4592959)
- Relevance: **Direct project component.** Combines manufacturing planning and scheduling with negotiation between local agents.

### 3. Supply Chain Management: A Multi-Agent System for Collaborative Production Planning

- Authors: Wilhelm Dangelmaier, Jens Heidenreich, and Ulrich Pape
- Year and venue: 2005, IEEE International Conference on e-Technology, e-Commerce and e-Service
- DOI: [10.1109/EEE.2005.128](https://doi.org/10.1109/EEE.2005.128)
- Relevance: **Direct project component.** Supports decentralized production-plan and supplier coordination across separate supply-chain entities.

### 4. Agent-Based Distributed Manufacturing Process Planning and Scheduling: A State-of-the-Art Survey

- Authors: Weiming Shen, Lihui Wang, and Qi Hao
- Year and venue: 2006, IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics, Part C, 36(4), 563–577
- DOI: [10.1109/TSMCC.2006.874022](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSMCC.2006.874022)
- Relevance: **Direct project background.** Surveys agent-based process planning, scheduling, resource assignment, bidding, and market-style coordination.

### 5. Distributed Manufacturing Scheduling Using Intelligent Agents

- Author: Weiming Shen
- Year and venue: 2002, IEEE Intelligent Systems, 17(1), 88–94
- DOI: [10.1109/5254.988492](https://doi.org/10.1109/5254.988492)
- Relevance: **Direct project component.** Explains distributed scheduling with intelligent agents when jobs and manufacturing resources change dynamically.

### 6. A Complete Multiagent Framework for Robust and Adaptable Dynamic Job Shop Scheduling

- Authors: Ning Liu, Mohamed A. Abdelrahman, and Srini Ramaswamy
- Year and venue: 2007, IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics, Part C, 37(5), 904–916
- DOI: [10.1109/TSMCC.2007.900658](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSMCC.2007.900658)
- Relevance: **Direct project component.** Useful for disruption response, changing jobs, rescheduling, and robust agent behavior.

### 7. Agent-Based Interaction Protocols and Topologies for Manufacturing Task Allocation

- Authors: Mohammad Owliya et al.
- Year and venue: 2013, IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics: Systems, 43(1), 38–52
- DOI: [10.1109/TSMCA.2012.2192263](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSMCA.2012.2192263)
- Relevance: **Direct negotiation reference.** Compares Contract Net arrangements and interaction topologies for manufacturing task allocation.

### 8. Dynamic Resource Task Negotiation to Enable Product Agent Exploration in Multi-Agent Manufacturing Systems

- Authors: Ilya Kovalenko et al.
- Year and venue: 2019, IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters, 4(3), 2854–2861
- DOI: [10.1109/LRA.2019.2921947](https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2019.2921947)
- Relevance: **Direct negotiation reference.** Studies product-agent and resource-agent negotiation without requiring one global factory model.

### 9. Decentralized Tabu Searches in Multi Agent System for Distributed and Flexible Job Shop Scheduling Problem

- Authors: Bilel Marzouki, Olfa Belkahla Driss, and Khaled Ghédira
- Year and venue: 2017, IEEE/ACS International Conference on Computer Systems and Applications, 1019–1026
- DOI: [10.1109/AICCSA.2017.133](https://doi.org/10.1109/AICCSA.2017.133)
- Relevance: **Supporting reference.** Demonstrates decentralized agent scheduling across distributed factories and machines.

### 10. Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning for Real-Time Dynamic Production Scheduling in a Robot Assembly Cell

- Authors: Daniel Johnson, Guodong Chen, and Yuqian Lu
- Year and venue: 2022, IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters, 7(3), 7684–7691
- DOI: [10.1109/LRA.2022.3184795](https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2022.3184795)
- Relevance: **Supporting advanced reference.** Shows a newer decentralized scheduling method, but reinforcement learning is outside the planned core.

## Taylor & Francis

### 11. Modelling and simulation of MRP II activities in multi agent systems

- Authors: S. Turgay, C. Kubat, and H. Taşkın
- Year and journal: 2007, Production Planning & Control, 18(1), 25–34
- DOI: [10.1080/09537280600940663](https://doi.org/10.1080/09537280600940663)
- Relevance: **Closest direct match.** Models integrated make-to-order MRP II activities with multiple agents, including purchasing and manufacturing orders.

### 12. An agent-based negotiation approach to integrate process planning and scheduling

- Authors: T. N. Wong et al.
- Year and journal: 2006, International Journal of Production Research, 44(7), 1331–1351
- DOI: [10.1080/00207540500409723](https://doi.org/10.1080/00207540500409723)
- Relevance: **Direct negotiation reference.** Part and machine agents bid through a modified Contract Net Protocol to allocate routings and resources.

### 13. Integrated shop floor control using autonomous agents

- Authors: Grace Yuh-Jiun Lin and James J. Solberg
- Year and journal: 1992, IIE Transactions, 24(3), 57–71
- DOI: [10.1080/07408179208964224](https://doi.org/10.1080/07408179208964224)
- Relevance: **Direct project component.** Uses market-like negotiation between parts and manufacturing resources under changing conditions and machine failures.

### 14. Auction-theoretic coordination of production planning in the supply chain

- Authors: Kadir Ertogral and S. David Wu
- Year and journal: 2000, IIE Transactions, 32(10), 931–940
- DOI: [10.1080/07408170008967451](https://doi.org/10.1080/07408170008967451)
- Relevance: **Direct planning reference.** Decomposes production planning among autonomous supply-chain entities coordinated through auctions.
