# How to legally download the shortlisted papers

Prepared: 2026-07-18

The collection now has 35 papers: 20 IEEE, 9 Elsevier, 4 Taylor & Francis, and 2 Springer. Use the following workflow for each DOI.

## Fastest workflow

1. Copy the DOI from the paper list.
2. Open `https://doi.org/DOI` after replacing `DOI` with the copied value.
3. First connect through the college library portal, campus Wi-Fi, or the college VPN/EZproxy.
4. On the publisher page, use the institutional sign-in option and select the college.
5. When access is recognized, open the PDF and use the browser's download button.

A free personal publisher account normally does not unlock a subscription paper. College/library access is the best first option.

## IEEE Xplore

1. Open the DOI or search the exact title in IEEE Xplore.
2. Select **Institutional Sign-In**.
3. Find the college through Shibboleth, OpenAthens, or the organization list.
4. Complete the college login.
5. Select **PDF**, then download or save it from the PDF viewer.

If the page says **Open Access**, the PDF can be downloaded directly.

## Elsevier / ScienceDirect

1. Open the DOI or search the title on ScienceDirect.
2. Select **Check access through your organization** or **Sign in via your institution**.
3. Select the college and complete its login.
4. Select the PDF icon or **View PDF**.
5. Use **Download** or **Save** in the PDF viewer.

## SpringerLink

1. Open the DOI or search the title on SpringerLink.
2. If locked, select **Log in via an institution**.
3. Select the college and complete the login.
4. Select **Download PDF** when the page shows full access.

An open-access paper can be downloaded directly. A legitimate Springer **SharedIt** link may provide full-text read access, although it may be view-only.

## Taylor & Francis Online

1. Open the DOI or search the exact title on Taylor & Francis Online.
2. Select **Access through your institution** or **Shibboleth**.
3. Select the college and complete its login.
4. Select **PDF** or **EPUB**, then save the file.

Open-access articles can be downloaded without a subscription.

## If the college does not provide access

Try these legal options in order:

1. Install the free Unpaywall browser extension and reopen the DOI page. Its green tab indicates a legal repository or author-manuscript copy.
2. Search the exact title or DOI on the author's university profile or institutional repository. Look for an **accepted manuscript**. Its formatting may differ, but it contains the peer-reviewed content.
3. Submit the DOI through the college library's **Interlibrary Loan** or **Document Delivery** form.
4. Email the corresponding author and politely request an author-approved manuscript or sharing link.
5. Purchase the paper only as a last resort.

Suggested email:

> I am a computer-engineering student working on decentralized MRP II. My institution does not provide access to your paper, "TITLE" (DOI). Could you please share an author-approved manuscript or legal access link for academic use?

## Avoid

- Do not use shared institutional passwords.
- Do not use Sci-Hub, LibGen, paywall-bypass extensions, or pirated copies.
- Do not automate bulk downloads from publisher sites.
- Do not publicly upload subscription PDFs.

These actions can violate copyright or the college's publisher-access agreement.
