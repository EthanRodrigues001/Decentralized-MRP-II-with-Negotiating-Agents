These papers are references only. They do not control the build architecture or replace the deterministic MRP II rules.

## 26. A Multi-Agent System for Manufacturing Material Resource Planning

- Authors: Yujun Zheng, Kan Wang, and Junwei Yang
- Published: 2006, Sixth International Conference on Intelligent Systems Design and Applications
- DOI: `10.1109/ISDA.2006.253769`
- IEEE Xplore document: `4021821`
- Relevance: The closest addition to the MRP layer. It connects manufacturing material-resource planning with a multi-agent system, while this project extends the idea into broader MRP II functions.

## 27. Coordination Mechanisms for Multi-Agent Manufacturing Systems: Applications to Integrated Manufacturing Scheduling

- Authors: R. Sikora and M. J. Shaw
- Published: 1997, IEEE Transactions on Engineering Management, 44(2), 175-187
- DOI: `10.1109/17.584925`
- IEEE Xplore document: `584925`
- Relevance: Explains how separate manufacturing agents coordinate decisions and applies the approach to integrated scheduling.

## 28. Agent Cooperation Mechanism for Decentralized Manufacturing Scheduling

- Authors: Guiovanni Jules and Mozafar Saadat
- Published: 2017, IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics: Systems, 47(12), 3351-3362
- DOI: `10.1109/TSMC.2016.2578879`
- IEEE Xplore document: `7583665`
- Relevance: Very close to the proposed system. Local manufacturing units keep control of their own schedules while agents cooperate to produce a workable combined schedule.

## 29. Constraint-Based Winner Determination for Auction-Based Scheduling

- Authors: Chun Wang, H. H. Ghenniwa, and Weiming Shen
- Published: 2009, IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics - Part A, 39(3), 609-618
- DOI: `10.1109/TSMCA.2009.2013194`
- IEEE Xplore document: `4787113`
- Relevance: Useful for the negotiation engine because it shows how to select winning bids without violating scheduling constraints.

## 30. An Adaptive Negotiation Framework for Agent Based Dynamic Manufacturing Scheduling

- Authors: Chun Wang, Weiming Shen, and H. Ghenniwa
- Published: 2003, IEEE International Conference on Systems, Man and Cybernetics, volume 2, 1211-1216
- DOI: `10.1109/ICSMC.2003.1244576`
- IEEE Xplore document: `1244576`
- Relevance: Direct support for offers, counteroffers, and renewed negotiation when orders or factory conditions change.

## 31. A Multi-Contract Net Protocol for Dynamic Scheduling in Flexible Manufacturing Systems (FMS)

- Authors: D. Ouelhadj, C. Hanachi, B. Bouzouia, A. Moualek, and A. Farhi
- Published: 1999, IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation, volume 2, 1114-1119
- DOI: `10.1109/ROBOT.1999.772469`
- IEEE Xplore document: `772469`
- Relevance: A direct Contract Net reference for task announcements, bids, awards, and dynamic rescheduling in manufacturing.

## 32. Modeling and Control of Holonic Manufacturing Systems Based on Extended Contract Net Protocol

- Author: Fu-Shiung Hsieh
- Published: 2002, American Control Conference, volume 6, 5037-5042
- DOI: `10.1109/ACC.2002.1025464`
- IEEE Xplore document: `1025464`
- Relevance: Shows how autonomous manufacturing units can use an extended Contract Net protocol to coordinate and allocate resources.

## 33. Holonic Job Shop Scheduling Using a Multiagent System

- Authors: S. S. Walker, R. W. Brennan, and D. H. Norrie
- Published: 2005, IEEE Intelligent Systems, 20(1), 50-57
- DOI: `10.1109/MIS.2005.9`
- IEEE Xplore document: `1392674`
- Relevance: Useful for modelling autonomous order and resource agents in a practical job-shop scheduling layer.

## 34. iShopFloor: An Internet-Enabled Agent-Based Intelligent Shop Floor

- Authors: W. Shen, S. Y. T. Lang, and L. Wang
- Published: 2005, IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics, Part C, 35(3), 371-381
- DOI: `10.1109/TSMCC.2004.843224`
- IEEE Xplore document: `1487585`
- Relevance: Supports the shop-floor feedback and distributed-control side of closed-loop MRP II.

## 35. Auction-Based Job Scheduling for Smart Manufacturing

- Authors: Emil Gatial, Zoltan Balogh, Sepideh Hassankhani Dolatabadi, Hatem Ghorbel, Stefano Carrino, Jonathan Dreyer, Vicente Rodriguez Montequin, Adrian Gligor, and Laszlo Barna Iantovics
- Published: 2023, IEEE 17th International Symposium on Applied Computational Intelligence and Informatics, 693-698
- DOI: `10.1109/SACI58269.2023.10158649`
- IEEE Xplore document: `10158649`
- Relevance: A newer example of auction-based job allocation and scheduling in smart manufacturing.
