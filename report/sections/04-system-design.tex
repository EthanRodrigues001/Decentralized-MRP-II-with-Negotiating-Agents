\section{Agent Roles}

\begin{description}[nosep]
  \item[Order Agent] Owns one production order. Explodes its bill of
        materials, requests offers, scores them, and holds the resulting
        commitments.
  \item[Inventory Agent] Owns current stock. Offers the quantity it can
        reserve and holds reservations.
  \item[Supplier Agent] Represents one supplier. Offers missing material at a
        price and delivery date.
  \item[Work-Centre Agent] Owns one work centre's finite capacity. Offers
        production slots.
  \item[Explanation Agent] Produces read-only natural-language summaries of a
        recorded negotiation. Holds no planning authority.
\end{description}

\section{Data Ownership and Storage}

Each agent owns a private slice of state that only it may write. Shared
reference data is read-only to agents. All consequential events are appended to
an audit log that is never updated in place, which is what makes the decision
trace reconstructable after the fact.
